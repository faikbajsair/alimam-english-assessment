Simple Steps to Manage English Assessments

Learn how to create standardized international question banks, configure strict anti-cheating proctoring, apply standardized rubric grading, and issue digital certificates.

1

Building Question Banks for TOEFL, IELTS, or EPT

Open the Question Bank & Test Builder tab in the admin dashboard. Select the desired section (Listening, Structure, Reading, Writing, or Speaking). You can input audio file URLs (MP3/OGG) for Listening, or set minimum word counts and analytical rubrics for Writing Task 2.

Tip: You can also bulk import hundreds of questions at once using the Export & Import CSV Template feature.
2

Proctoring Supervision & Exam Security (Anti-Cheat)

During exams, candidates must grant webcam permissions and remain in full-screen mode. The system automatically:

  • Disables right-click, text selection, and shortcut combinations (`Ctrl+C`, `Ctrl+V`, `F12`).
  • Detects tab switching or external application focus with a 3-Strike Warning System before auto-submission.
  • Captures periodic webcam snapshots to verify candidate identity and integrity throughout the exam.
3

Manual Speaking & Writing Grading (Standard Assessment Rubrics)

Examiners can access the Grading Queue to review essay responses and playback candidate voice recordings (Speaking Part 2). Grade responses across 4 criteria: Task Achievement, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, and Grammatical Accuracy, along with student academic adab/character notes.

4

Certificate Issuance & Assessment Performance Reports

Once grading is finalized, the system converts raw scores to official TOEFL scales (310–677) or IELTS Bands (0–9.0) with CEFR competency mapping (A1–C2). Official PDF certificates can be downloaded and verified globally via QR Code.
